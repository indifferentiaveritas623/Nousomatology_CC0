>Materialism

1.Discription :
a form of monism holding that matter is the fundamental substance of nature, so that all things, including mind and consciousness, arise from material interactions and depend on physical processes, including those of the human brain and nervous system.
it asserts that the real world consists of only material things

1.2.Is this a element of Ontology or Epistemology or else if else, what ?

Ontology : monist ontology 

-variations / association

1.Naïve / Spontaneous Materialism: the world as a physical whole without sophisticated scientific backing. 
2.Mechanical / Metaphysical Materialism: the universe as a giant machine made of tiny, hard particles (atoms) interacting strictly through physical movement and cause-and-effect. 
3.Dialectical Materialism: The framework developed by Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels that applies dialectical change to the material world, viewing nature and society as interconnected, changing systems. 
4.Historical Materialism: Karl Marx’s theory stating that society's economic and material conditions—rather than ideals or culture—drive historical evolution and social change. 

5.reductionism : the practice of analyzing and explaining complex systems, ideas, or phenomena by breaking them down into their simpler, more fundamental constituent parts

6.determinism : all events—including human actions and choices—are completely determined by previously existing causes and the laws of nature

7.Reductive Materialism (Identity Theory): mental states (like thoughts or pain) are real, but they are identical to specific physical brain states and processes. 

8.Eliminative Materialism: everyday psychological concepts (like "beliefs" or "desires") are false, and that an advanced neuroscience will eventually eliminate them entirely rather than reduce them
